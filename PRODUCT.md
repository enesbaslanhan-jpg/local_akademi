## Platform

web

## Users

LocalKarar serves small-business owners and operators who need to understand their current business state, make consequential decisions, learn practical skills, and keep daily records without switching between unrelated tools.

## Product Purpose

LocalKarar combines operational control, decision support, finance workflows, learning, analytical models, grounded AI mentoring, business tracking, and relevant editorial/community context in one coherent workspace. Success means the user can see what matters now, understand why, and take the next safe action.

## Positioning

The product joins three normally separate modes of work: operating the business, learning what to do, and producing a reviewable decision record. The differentiator is the continuity between context, reasoning, action, and durable evidence.

## Operating Context

Users work in short daily check-ins, focused decision sessions, longer learning sessions, finance and model-analysis workflows, and record-management tasks. The interface must support both compact data scanning and calm reading without changing product identity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Eighteen primary desktop surfaces form one product family.
- Turkish product terminology, existing routes, information architecture, data behavior, and application dependencies remain unchanged during this design phase.
- The current task creates design artifacts only. Application code, Git staging, commits, and pushes are out of scope.
- Motion is documented as interaction vocabulary only and must not introduce a runtime dependency.
- Values shown in mockups are illustrative unless already present in supplied product sources. Unsupported claims and fabricated precision are prohibited.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: LocalKarar.
- Approved direction: Premium Operational + Apple Calm Depth.
- Approved palette: Warm Petrol.
- Primary type family: Manrope.
- Product mode: Operate first; Read where learning and editorial content require it.
- Pure-white canvas dominance, saturated blue/cyan, decorative green, generic admin styling, card-everything layouts, and ornamental glass/neon are explicitly rejected.

## Product Principles

1. Show current state, meaning, and next action together.
2. Keep the family unified through tokens and behavior while giving each workflow its own anatomy.
3. Preserve user agency with visible context, recoverable progress, and durable decision evidence.
4. Earn premium quality through hierarchy, typography, material restraint, and precise feedback.
5. Prefer real operational content and honest uncertainty over decorative dashboards or invented metrics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, logical focus order, 44px touch targets, non-color status cues, text scaling, reduced motion, and reduced transparency alternatives are required.
