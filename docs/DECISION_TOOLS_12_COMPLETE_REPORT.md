## Tool-by-tool implementation

### 1. DC-PROFIT-001 — Ürünüm Gerçekten Kârlı mı?

- Inputs: sale price, product cost, commission, shipping, packaging, return allowance, other variable cost, discount.
- Calculations: net sale price, commission, total variable cost, contribution, contribution margin, break-even price and safety checks.
- Thresholds: positive contribution, positive margin, break-even and missing-information blockers from the existing profitability rule engine.
- Snapshot: existing profitability result schema remains unchanged.
- Mentor summary: existing safe profitability summary remains unchanged.
- Validation: existing unit/API tests plus real PostgreSQL completion passed.
- Limitation: tax, fixed overhead allocation and accounting policy remain outside this quick decision check.

### 2. DC-DISCOUNT-002 — Bu indirimi yapabilir miyim?

- Inputs: sale price, unit cost, commission, planned discount, current monthly units, expected sales lift.
- Formulas: discounted price; contribution after cost and commission; campaign break-even units.
- Thresholds: discounted contribution must remain positive; expected lift must replace lost margin; planned discount is checked against the safe limit.
- Snapshot/Mentor: current versus discounted contribution, required lift, expected campaign contribution, warnings and a staged discount test plan.
- Validation: exact Wave 1 calculation test and real desktop form-to-result flow passed.
- Limitation: demand lift is an estimate and should be confirmed with a capped pilot.

### 3. DC-FREESHIP-003 — Kargo ücretsiz olabilir mi?

- Inputs: basket value, basket product cost, commission, packaging, carrier cost, customer shipping fee, items per basket.
- Formulas: current basket contribution; free-shipping contribution; safe minimum basket threshold.
- Thresholds: free-shipping contribution must be positive and the basket must clear a 10% safety-margin threshold.
- Snapshot/Mentor: current/free-shipping comparison, contribution loss, safe basket threshold and shipping-policy actions.
- Validation: exact Wave 1 calculation test and real 375 px form-to-result flow passed.
- Limitation: carrier zones, dimensional weight and return shipping require separate operational review.

### 4. DC-MARKETPLACE-004 — Pazaryeri komisyonundan sonra ne kalıyor?

- Inputs: marketplace price, product cost, commission, service fees, ad cost, seller shipping, packaging, direct-channel fee rate.
- Formulas: net marketplace collection; product contribution; minimum viable marketplace price.
- Thresholds: positive contribution, price above minimum and marketplace-versus-direct channel contribution gap.
- Snapshot/Mentor: marketplace net, all-in deductions, direct-channel comparison and channel-pricing actions.
- Validation: exact Wave 1 calculation test and real PostgreSQL/API completion passed.
- Limitation: platform-specific tax and campaign subsidies must be entered or reviewed separately.

### 5. DC-ADS-005 — Reklam bütçemi artırmalı mıyım?

- Inputs: ad spend, attributed revenue/orders, pre-ad order contribution, conversion, planned budget increase, expected efficiency change.
- Formulas: ROAS; post-ad contribution; incremental-budget contribution.
- Thresholds: positive current contribution, ROAS above break-even and positive contribution after expected efficiency loss.
- Snapshot/Mentor: ROAS, CPA, break-even ROAS, current/incremental contribution and controlled scale-up actions.
- Validation: unique Wave 2 metric test and real desktop form-to-result flow passed.
- Limitation: attribution windows and organic cannibalization are not inferred automatically.

### 6. DC-HIRE-006 — Yeni personel alabilir miyim?

- Inputs: gross salary, employer on-cost, other monthly cost, expected added revenue, contribution margin, free cash, cash reserve.
- Formulas: full employment cost; break-even added revenue; reserve coverage in months.
- Thresholds: expected contribution and free cash must cover cost; reserve target is at least three months; a 30% lower-revenue scenario is checked.
- Snapshot/Mentor: total employer cost, expected contribution, break-even revenue, reserve months and hiring gates.
- Validation: unique Wave 2 metric test and real PostgreSQL/API completion passed.
- Limitation: payroll, employment-law and local benefit calculations require professional confirmation.

### 7. DC-LOAN-007 — Kredi taksitini karşılayabilir miyim?

- Inputs: new installment, free cash, existing debt payments, cash reserve, term, downside cash drop.
- Formulas: debt-service coverage ratio; monthly cash buffer; downside coverage ratio.
- Thresholds: normal DSCR target 1.25; downside DSCR at least 1.00; reserve at least three total installments.
- Snapshot/Mentor: total monthly debt, normal/downside coverage, reserve coverage and borrowing safeguards.
- Validation: unique Wave 2 metric test and real 375 px form-to-result flow passed.
- Limitation: interest changes, covenants and total legal repayment cost require lender-document review.

### 8. DC-CASHFLOW-008 — Nakit akışım riskli mi?

- Inputs: opening cash, monthly inflow/outflow, receivable days, payable days, minimum cash buffer.
- Formulas: monthly net flow; timing-gap financing need; cash runway.
- Thresholds: adjusted cash cannot be negative; negative-flow runway should be at least three months; minimum buffer must remain intact.
- Snapshot/Mentor: net flow, timing gap, adjusted cash, runway, buffer gap and 13-week cash-plan actions.
- Validation: unique Wave 2 metric test and real PostgreSQL/API completion passed.
- Limitation: seasonal and one-off cash movements must be reflected by the user in the inputs.

### 9. DC-BRANCH-009 — Yeni şube açmaya hazır mıyım?

- Inputs: initial investment, monthly fixed cost, expected revenue, contribution margin, reserved cash, ramp-up months, downside revenue drop.
- Formulas: monthly operating result; break-even revenue; investment payback period.
- Thresholds: positive expected result; adequate investment plus ramp-up reserve; positive downside result; payback warning above 36 months.
- Snapshot/Mentor: normal/downside result, break-even, payback, reserve need/gap and pilot-location actions.
- Validation: unique Wave 3 comparison test and real desktop form-to-result flow passed.
- Limitation: local demand, lease clauses and cannibalization need separate due diligence.

### 10. DC-CAMPAIGN-010 — Kampanya yapmak mantıklı mı?

- Inputs: current price, variable cost, discount, fixed campaign cost, baseline units, low/medium/high sales-lift estimates.
- Formulas: campaign contribution; break-even units; scenario contribution versus normal-period contribution.
- Thresholds: positive discounted unit contribution; medium scenario must beat baseline; low-scenario loss is surfaced.
- Snapshot/Mentor: low/medium/high scenarios, break-even quantity, baseline comparison and stop rules.
- Validation: unique Wave 3 comparison test and real PostgreSQL/API completion passed.
- Limitation: stock capacity and campaign spillover beyond the measured period are not forecast automatically.

### 11. DC-STOCK-011 — Stok artırmalı mıyım?

- Inputs: current stock, average daily sales, lead time, demand variability, monthly unit holding cost, planned order quantity.
- Formulas: days of cover; reorder point; safety-stock-adjusted recommended order.
- Thresholds: stock must cover lead time; current stock is compared with reorder point; materially excessive planned orders trigger an overstock warning.
- Snapshot/Mentor: cover days, reorder point, recommended order, holding cost, projected days and reorder actions.
- Validation: unique Wave 3 comparison test and real 375 px form-to-result flow passed.
- Limitation: demand is treated as a recent-rate estimate; seasonality and supplier reliability need periodic input updates.

### 12. DC-CONTINUE-012 — Bu ürünü satmaya devam etmeli miyim?

- Inputs: sale price, variable cost, monthly units, return rate, loss per return, monthly holding cost, alternative-product contribution.
- Formulas: return-adjusted unit contribution; net monthly contribution; opportunity difference versus alternative.
- Thresholds: non-positive unit/monthly contribution produces `BIRAK`; lower opportunity value or return rate of at least 20% produces `GÖZDEN GEÇİR`; otherwise `DEVAM`.
- Snapshot/Mentor: current versus alternative contribution, return loss, opportunity gap and stop/test/continue actions.
- Validation: unique Wave 3 comparison test and real PostgreSQL/API completion passed.
- Limitation: strategic assortment value, customer acquisition effects and tax are not modeled.
