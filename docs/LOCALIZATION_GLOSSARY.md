## Financial distinctions

- `Gelir` → `Revenue`: recognized business income in a financial context.
- `Brüt satış` → `Gross Sales`: sales before discounts, refunds, fees, and other deductions.
- `Ciro` → `Turnover`: use only when the source intentionally means total business turnover; do not replace it with Revenue or Gross Sales.
- `Net katkı` → `Net Contribution`: value after the deductions available in LocalKarar; it is not net profit.
- Amounts always use the record/workspace currency. Never infer TRY from interface language.

Provider names, `LocalKarar`, and `AI Mentor` remain unchanged. Catalog data is not machine-translated.
